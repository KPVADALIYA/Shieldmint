# ShieldMint Insurance Advisory

**ShieldMint** is a professional, IRDAI-compliant insurance advisory platform led by **Kishan Vadaliya** based in Surat, Gujarat, serving clients across India. The platform provides unbiased policy comparison, transparent guidance, and dedicated claim assistance for Health, Term Life, Motor, Property, and Business Insurance.

Designed with a focus on conversion rate optimization (CRO), accessibility, and local SEO, ShieldMint eliminates insurance jargon and empowers clients to make informed financial protection decisions.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Interactive Components

- **⚖️ Advisory Comparison Matrix:** Interactive filterable comparison table contrasting unassisted direct online portals with ShieldMint's human-guided advisory across Health, Term Life, Motor, and SME Business categories.
- **📋 Interactive Claim Readiness Desk:** Step-by-step claim timelines and dynamic document checklists for Health/Mediclaim, Motor Accidents, Term Life payouts, and Business/Property claims with a real-time progress tracker.
- **🧮 Coverage Benchmark Calculator:** Interactive profile stage estimator allowing users to calculate recommended coverage amounts for Health, Term Life, Motor, and Business risks before requesting a consultation.
- **📍 Local & Pan-India SEO Hub:** Structured local service area tags covering Surat zones (*Adajan, Vesu, Pal, City Light, Piplod, Althan, Varachha, Katargam, Udhna, Textile Markets, GIDC*) as well as major Gujarat and pan-India economic hubs.
- **💬 Direct WhatsApp & Email Integration:** Instant inquiry form formatting that generates pre-filled WhatsApp messages or email drafts with single-click copy-to-clipboard fallbacks.
- **🔍 Structured Schema.json-LD:** Comprehensive LocalBusiness, FinancialService, InsuranceAgency, FAQPage, and HowTo schema markup for maximum search engine visibility.

---

## 💻 Tech Stack & Architecture

ShieldMint follows a **Single-File Architecture** for lightning-fast performance, zero build-step complexity, and effortless static hosting:

- **HTML5:** Semantic markup with strict accessibility (ARIA attributes, skip-links, and screen-reader support).
- **CSS3:** Custom design system utilizing CSS Custom Properties (`--green`, `--mint`, `--bg`), fluid typography (`clamp`), CSS Grid, and responsive media queries.
- **Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+):** Modular interactivity including Intersection Observer reveal animations, dynamic tab controllers, calculators, FAQ accordions, and clipboard utilities.
- **Icons & Graphics:** Inline optimized SVG icons and vector brand marks.
